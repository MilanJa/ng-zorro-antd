## zh-CN

`nzVariant` 设置快速跳转与简洁模式输入框的变体，未设置时取外层 `nz-form` 的 `nzVariant`。需要 v6 样式表。Ant Design 没有对应属性，变体来自 `ConfigProvider` 或表单。

## en-US

`nzVariant` sets the variant of the quick-jumper and simple-pager inputs; unset, it follows the enclosing `nz-form`'s `nzVariant`. Requires the v6 stylesheet. Ant Design has no such prop: the variant comes from `ConfigProvider` or the form.
