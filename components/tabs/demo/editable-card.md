## zh-CN

只有卡片样式的页签支持新增和关闭选项。本示例用 `nzKey` 和 `[(nzActiveKey)]` 按 key 选中页签，关闭当前页签时根据 `(nzClose)` 返回的 `key` 选中相邻页签。

## en-US

Only card type Tabs support adding & closable. This demo selects tabs by key with `nzKey` and `[(nzActiveKey)]`; closing the active tab selects its neighbour from the `key` that `(nzClose)` reports.
